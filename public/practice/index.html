Welcome to Puente

Five ways to cross the bridge from English-thinking to Spanish-thinking.

🧬 Fusion Mode

  1. Say each English word's Spanish translation out loud, then click it to flip and verify.
  2. Green words are already Spanish. When everything is green, hear the full sentence.
  3. Cards return sooner or later depending on how well you do (spaced repetition).
Estaba pensando about mudarme to la costa next year

👂 Echo Dictation

  • Hear a full Spanish sentence, type what you hear. You get a word-by-word diff and a match score. 80%+ passes.

🧩 Sentence Builder

  • Hear the sentence, then rebuild it by clicking shuffled word chips in the right order.

✏️ Cloze Quiz

  • A fully-Spanish sentence with one gap — produce the missing word from memory.

🎤 Speak Back

  • Repeat the sentence aloud; your browser's speech recognition scores your pronunciation. (Chrome/Edge recommended.)

📈 Progress

  • Choose Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced above the card, or mix all three levels.
  • Earn XP for every correct action; level up as you go.
  • Each card has a review level ●●○○○ — higher levels come back less often.
  • Every sentence carries a 📘 grammar note, shown after you complete it.
  • Filter topics and tune audio in ⚙️ Settings.
