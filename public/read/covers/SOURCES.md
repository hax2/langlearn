# Cover artwork

The nine WebP covers below were generated for this project on 2026-08-15 with
OpenAI's image-generation tool, using the restored cover set only as a broad
mood reference. The working direction was a portrait 2:3, painterly cinematic
illustration with no typography, logos, borders, or watermarks.

- `el_leon_y_el_raton.webp` — a lion caught in a rope net while a mouse gnaws
  through the cord in a warm woodland.
- `el_cuervo_y_el_zorro.webp` — a crow, falling cheese, and a flattering fox
  beneath an old oak at golden hour.
- `la_cigarra_y_la_hormiga.webp` — a singing cicada and a grain-carrying ant at
  the seasonal boundary between summer and winter.
- `bebe_y_el_senor_don_pomposo.webp` — a boy in a sailor suit quietly gives his
  golden toy sword to his sleeping cousin in a nineteenth-century nursery.
- `nene_traviesa.webp` — Nené beside the torn pages of an enormous illustrated
  natural-history book, with her father in the doorway.
- `la_muneca_negra.webp` — a girl embraces her worn black cloth doll while a
  pristine porcelain doll remains in the background.
- `los_ojos_verdes.webp` — a hunter at a forbidden moonlit spring meets the
  mysterious woman with luminous green eyes.
- `el_miserere.webp` — spectral monks sing in a storm-struck ruined monastery
  as a composer watches.
- `maese_perez_el_organista.webp` — the blind organist plays in a candlelit
  Seville church on Christmas Eve.

The generated PNG masters remain in the local Codex image-output directory;
the repository versions are centered 848 × 1264 WebP derivatives optimized for
the catalog.

The six Bible covers were generated for this project on 2026-08-15 with the same
tool and no external artwork. They use symbolic, text-free scenes: a shepherd's
valley for selected Psalms, a lit crossroads for selected Proverbs, a tree through
four seasons for Ecclesiastes, two women in a barley field for Ruth, a ship and
great fish for Jonah, and a hillside gathering for the Sermon on the Mount. The
repository files are centered 848 × 1264 WebP derivatives of the generated PNGs.

The five Greek-classics covers were generated for this project on 2026-08-15
with the same tool and no external artwork. They depict Epicurus teaching in a
garden, the fire and exit of Plato's cave, Socrates speaking with Crito in prison,
Odysseus escaping beneath the Cyclops' rams, and the ship passing the Sirens,
Scylla, and Charybdis. They contain no typography and are stored as centered
848 × 1264 WebP derivatives.
